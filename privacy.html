Privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026. Personal Assistant local Gmail access is a private, single-user app. It processes only owner-authorized personal Google Workspace data for personal assistance.

The message archive feature is under development, not active. It processes message text, identifiers, participants, timestamps and available images. Separate drive.file authorization permits access to app-created or explicitly selected files. Credentials and temporary staging stay private locally; exports go to private Google Drive folders. Owner-authorized services may read those exports.

Recognized sign-in codes and links are redacted from exported text; detection is not guaranteed. Image pixels are not redacted. Archives remain until the owner requests deletion. Temporary staging is removed when recovery permits; minimal integrity records remain. Revoking Google authorization stops future access but does not delete existing exports.

This information site receives no app data and uses no app analytics, scripts or sign-in forms. GitHub Pages may log visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions, use the support contact on the Google consent screen. App overview
